About the school

Encourage. Educate. Empower.

We believe that quality education is the key to a quality life.

Kautilya School in Kota gives children a supportive, nurturing place to grow, academically and personally: traditional values with modern teaching, digital classrooms, labs, music and dance, and sport.

The school is run by Sugra Shikshan Sansthan, Kota, and is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education.
